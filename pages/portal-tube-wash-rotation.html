Tube Wash: Rotating Bundle

How the tube bundle is washed in the vessel. The fan stays put, the bundle turns.

Bundle rotation, one full turn

The lance is held steady at the bore. Each tube cleans as it carries through the spray, so one revolution clears the bundle.

What you are looking at

  • Fan position. Tip in at the bore center with the fan flat to the tube faces. Do not chase individual tubes with the wand.
  • Rotation. Turn the bundle so the front row travels left to right at a steady pace. Front tubes carry through the spray, then wrap around the back.
  • Coverage. Set a start mark before you begin and stop on the same mark. One full revolution washes every tube face.
  • Result. Media clears off the tube faces as they pass. Anything still coated after a full turn means the pass was too fast or the fan was off the faces.
  • The plan view at the bottom of the drawing shows the geometry: fixed fan at the front, bundle rotating past it.
