What you can text or ask

Text a question directly and the AI searches the NBTS knowledge base and answers in seconds — or use a keyword to open a ticket or add to an existing one.

Text these keywords

Menu See every option at any time.
Start ticket Opens a tracked Jira ticket. Include your name, email and the issue — for example: Name: Jane Smith, Email: jane@company.com, Issue: Need RMF 12 wire guide
Leave comment Adds to a ticket you already have. Include Ticket: NBTS-### and your comment — you can attach photos, which land right on the ticket.

No keyword needed for a question — just text it and the AI will look it up.

Specs, parts & compatibility

What's the flow rate on an SHF sand high-rate filter?
Is this strainer compatible with a 6-inch line?

The AI answers only from approved NBTS documentation — it won't guess at specs or part numbers. If it doesn't have the answer, it hands you to the team.

Not sure which product you have? Just describe it. The AI identifies the brand and model from what you write — and if it's still unclear (say, which Defender controller you're on), it asks one quick question rather than guessing. Reply Start ticket any time to reach a live engineer.
