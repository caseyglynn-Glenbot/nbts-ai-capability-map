Menu See every option at any time.

Leave comment Adds to a ticket you already have. Include Ticket: NBTS-### and your comment. You can attach photos, which land right on the ticket.

Questions the AI can answer

Troubleshooting & alarms

My Defender is showing an alarm. What should I check first?
Pressure differential is climbing on our sand filter. What causes that?

Wiring & installation

What are the wiring connections for an RMF 12 controller?
I need the wire guide for an RMF 11.

Operation & maintenance

How do I backwash a Defender regenerative media filter?
What's the recommended media charge for this filter?

Specs, parts & compatibility

What's the flow rate on an SHF sand high-rate filter?
Is this strainer compatible with a 6-inch line?

The AI answers only from approved NBTS documentation. It won't guess at specs or part numbers. If it doesn't have the answer, it hands you to the team.

Not sure which product you have? Just describe it. The AI identifies the brand and model from what you write, and if it's still unclear (say, which Defender controller you're on), it asks one quick question rather than guessing. Reply Start ticket any time to reach a live engineer.

How the agent handles a call

Name the equipmentA model (Duron, HS730, ICEAS) narrows the search to that model; a brand word searches the whole brand. Two brands in one call and the agent asks which to start with.
Ask for a personOffers help once, then warm-transfers to the product group in order. After 3 failed attempts it promises a callback from the first contact.
Set up a callbackConfirms the time and names who will call.
Won't doGive warranty terms, direct confined-space entry (points to the plant permit process and lockout), or read contact numbers to callers.

Answers come only from the WI Projects knowledge base. It won't make up numbers; if it doesn't have the answer, it transfers or opens a ticket for the team.
