A flattened view of the same wash, built to show coverage rather than bundle motion. The pass runs left to right across the bundle, one lane at a time, so it is easy to point at where a miss happens and what it leaves behind.

COVERAGE, LANE BY LANE

Use this one to talk through technique and skipped lanes. For the way the bundle actually moves, use the rotation page.

WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
  1. Start left. Tip against the first row, valve open, fan flat to the tube faces.
  2. Work across. Part each lane so the back rows get washed, not just the faces you can see.
  3. Finish right. One continuous pass, no skipped lanes.
  4. Why it matters. A skipped lane does not show up at the vessel. It shows up later as carryover and short runs.
