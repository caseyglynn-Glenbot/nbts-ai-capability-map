How perlite media works

A cross section through one flexible tube, magnified from the bump cycle. Perlite builds a cake on the outside of the tube, debris collects on the faces pointed at the flow, and each bump turns the particles so a clean face takes over.

Cross section detail, one tube wall at roughly 400×

Filtering

Debris rides in with the influent and lodges against the perlite faces that meet the flow. Water keeps passing through into the tube.

Perlite is jagged and hollow, so the cake traps particles down to about 1 µm without a fine screen. Because each bump presents an unused face instead of flushing the media away, one charge of perlite keeps working through many cycles before it needs replacing.
