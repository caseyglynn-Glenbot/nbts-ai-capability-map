NBTS AI Platform

Knowledge Base Loader

Add product documentation to the AI knowledge base yourself — no engineering needed. Anything you load here is read, chunked, embedded, and made searchable by the Technical Support AI within minutes.

What you can upload

PDF documents
Manuals, cut sheets, spec sheets — including scanned, image-only files (OCR is applied automatically).
Engineering drawings
P&IDs and technical drawings in PDF — read page-by-page with AI vision, not just text extraction.
Word documents
.docx product write-ups, guides and technical notes.
Excel spreadsheets
.xlsx parts tables and data — flattened to text so the AI can read every row.
Google Docs
Paste a Google Doc link — pulled in and processed directly.
Markdown, HTML & text
.md, .html and .txt — notes, exported pages and plain text.

What happens when you upload

  • The document is read (with OCR for scans and AI vision for drawings), split into chunks, embedded, and stored in the Supabase vector store.
  • Re-uploading the same document replaces the old version — it won't create duplicates.
  • Once processed, the Technical Support AI can cite it when answering customer and field questions.
