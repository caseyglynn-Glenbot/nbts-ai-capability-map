WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
  1. Bump assembly. Lift shaft nut, bump tire, snap ring, bushing o-ring, bump bushing and bump gasket stack on the lift shaft at the center of the upper head. These come off before the head.
  2. Upper head and davit. The head bolts to the shell flange on the head gasket. The davit post on the shell carries the head so it swings clear without a lift.
  3. Tube sheet assembly. Hold down plate, felt gasket, tube sheet plate and the tube elements lift out together on the lift shaft. The lift shaft o-rings seal where the shaft passes through the head.
  4. Viewing window. Cover, acrylic and gasket on the front of the shell. Any time the cover is off, check the acrylic for crazing and the gasket for a set.
  5. Base unit. Shell, lower head, drain, influent and effluent connections and the legs stay put. Legs ship collapsed and extend on site.
