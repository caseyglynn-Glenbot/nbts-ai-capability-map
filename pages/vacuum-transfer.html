Vacuum Transfer: Valve Sequence
Contact: Casey Glynn Lead Engineer

Media loading on the vacuum transfer system, walked through in the order the controller asks for it. The animation runs controller steps 15 through 25, opening and closing each valve on cue and showing media pulled from the bag into the tank.

THE VALVE ORDER, STEPS 15 TO 25

Each step highlights the valve it touches. The strip underneath is the live state of all five valves, including the pump discharge valve that is not on this drawing.

WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
  1. Vacuum drain closes first. Nothing else happens until it is shut. Step 15.
  2. Transfer valve, then hose valve. Open the vacuum transfer valve before the vacuum hose valve, then put the hose in the bag. Steps 16 to 18.
  3. Vacuum on until the media is loaded. Watch the amount going in rather than the clock. Steps 19 and 20.
  4. Close in reverse. Vacuum hose valve, then vacuum transfer valve. Steps 21 and 22.
  5. Then set up for the fill. Precoat vent line, pump discharge, vacuum drain, in that order, before the tank is filled and the precoat cycle starts. Steps 23 to 25.
Names follow the controller steps. The vacuum drain valve here is the valve the system drawing labels the vacuum vent valve, plumbed to waste independently. Flag it if your unit is tagged differently and the page gets corrected.
