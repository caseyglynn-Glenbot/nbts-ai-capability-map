Lane by lane coverage

A flattened view of the wash pattern. Useful for showing coverage and skipped lanes rather than the real bundle motion.

What you are looking at

  • Coverage view. This version flattens the bundle so the wash pattern reads left to right. For how the bundle actually moves in the vessel, see the rotating bundle page.
  • Start left. Tip against the first row, valve open, fan flat to the tube faces.
  • Work across. Part each lane so the back rows get washed, not just the faces you can see.
  • Finish right. One continuous pass. Skipped lanes are the common miss and they show up later as carryover.
