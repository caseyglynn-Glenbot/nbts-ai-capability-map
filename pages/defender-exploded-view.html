Defender: Exploded View
Contact: Casey Glynn Lead Engineer

The Defender regenerative media filter as it ships, then every major part pulled apart in the order it comes off the unit. Bump assembly and upper head first, the tube sheet assembly out as one piece, and the viewing window off the front. Use it to name a part on the phone, or to show a customer what is behind the head before they open it.

BASE UNIT TO FULL EXPLODED VIEW

The unit starts assembled, comes apart, holds with every part labeled, then goes back together. The loop runs about 16 seconds.

WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
  1. Bump assembly. Lift shaft nut, bump tire, snap ring, bushing o-ring, bump bushing and bump gasket stack on the lift shaft at the top of the upper head. These come off before the head.
  2. Upper head. Bolted to the shell flange on a head gasket. The davit on the shell carries the head so it swings clear without a lift.
  3. Tube sheet assembly. Hold down plate, felt gasket, tube sheet plate and the tube elements lift out as one piece on the lift shaft. The lift shaft o-rings seal where the shaft passes through the head.
  4. Viewing window. Cover, acrylic and gasket on the front of the shell. Any time the cover is off, look at the acrylic for crazing and the gasket for a set.
  5. Base unit. Shell, lower head, drain valve, influent and effluent connections and the legs stay where they are. Legs ship collapsed and extend on site.
