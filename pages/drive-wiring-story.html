Wiring Animation

Three wiring stories connecting the Defender RMF12 panel and the GreenDrive VFD, played in sequence: the run confirm signal back from the drive's Relay 1 to B-24+ and T-17, the pump start/stop circuit from T-39 and T-40 to drive terminals 12 and 18, then the T-40 to T-58 jumper and the jog speed for precoat from T-57 to terminal 19. The loop runs about 43 seconds and repeats.

Before You Start

Warning

Unit must be de-energized while working in the panel.

  1. These instructions assume that power has been supplied to each panel in accordance with application specific power requirements.
  2. The flow meter should be properly grounded per flow meter instructions.
  3. Ensure that switch S202 is in the "On" position (flip A53 and A54 switch to the right). This is located behind the keypad; carefully remove the LCP to access the switches. See Appendix B in the source document.
  4. Before entering any parameters, perform a copy ALL TO LCP function to back up the VFD program into the LCP display. If the program in the VFD has a problem, perform a copy ALL FROM LCP to overwrite any errors. This also applies to backing up a good program verified to meet application specific requirements.
