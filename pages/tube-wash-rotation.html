Tube Wash: Rotating Bundle
Contact: Casey Glynn Lead Engineer

How the tube bundle is washed in the vessel. The lance is held steady at the bore and the bundle is turned past it, so every tube face carries through the spray in one revolution. This is the motion as it happens on the unit, including the way the tubes bunch and shrink as they swing around the back.

THE WASH, ONE FULL TURN

Fan fixed at the front, bundle rotating left to right. The plan view at the bottom shows the same thing from above.

WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
  1. Fan position. Tip in at the bore center with the fan flat to the tube faces. Do not chase individual tubes with the wand.
  2. Rotation. Turn the bundle so the front row travels left to right at a steady pace. Front tubes pass through the spray, then wrap around the back.
  3. Coverage. Set a start mark before you begin and stop on the same mark. One full revolution washes every tube face.
  4. Result. Media clears off the faces as they pass. Anything still coated after a full turn means the pass was too fast or the fan was off the faces.
